About me

I am an FWO postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Economics at the University of Antwerp. I hold a joint PhD in History and Economics from KU Leuven and UCLouvain.

My research is in economic history and the history of knowledge. I study how pre-modern universities, scholars, and printers shaped the spread of ideas, combining bibliographic and prosopographical data with network analysis. My dissertation reconstructs the publication network of the Old University of Louvain (1425–1797).

In earlier work I studied the effects of antimafia policies on property values in Italy.

New working paper: Flora, Cosmos, Salvatio: Pre-modern Academic Institutions and the Spread of Ideas
